# Cross-System Edge Forensics Result - 2026-11-01

## Decision

The current bottleneck is not a shortage of emitted setups. It is weak
directional specificity after realistic costs, controls, and chronological
confirmation. Broad pair expansion is paused. No strategy, lifecycle state, or
execution permission changes are authorized by this diagnostic.

## Evidence

### Non-EUR pair research

- 21 immutable studies cover AUD/USD, EUR/CHF, GBP/USD, NZD/USD, USD/CAD, and
  USD/JPY.
- Only 1 of 20 R-based studies had positive development expectancy.
- Only 1 study was positive in both development and chronological OOS.
- 19 studies failed control specificity and 20 failed OOS stability.
- All 21 failed cost stress; the average additional cost erosion was about
  0.100R for R-based studies.
- Two positive baselines were erased under their locked cost stresses.

This is evidence against another broad pair-pattern sweep. It is not evidence
that every possible strategy on every pair is unprofitable.

### EUR/USD trading-agent forward evidence

The current experiment epoch contains 117 independent baseline episodes:

- average net outcome: -0.119R;
- median net outcome: -1.000R;
- positive outcome rate: 35.0%;
- average MFE: 1.012R;
- average MAE: 1.023R;
- average entry-spread proxy: 0.209R.

Adding the entry-spread proxy back produces +0.090R, but this is not a valid
zero-cost reconstruction. It shows that cost and stop geometry are material.
The nearly symmetric favorable and adverse excursions show that direction and
timing remain the central problem: episodes often move enough, but not reliably
in the selected direction before the stop or horizon.

Two expanded mechanisms are provisionally positive after strict episode
deduplication:

| Mechanism | Episodes | Average net R | Status |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| HTF-aligned trend pullback continuation | 4 | +0.250 | Descriptive only |
| VWAP reclaim / mean reversion | 4 | +0.250 | Descriptive only |

Both are far below the predeclared 30-episode minimum and have no execution
authority. The remaining expanded mechanisms are negative in their strict
linked-episode views.

## Next Work

1. Keep collecting exact forward evidence for the three EUR/USD lifecycle
   profiles without broadening their definitions.
2. Keep the HTF pullback and VWAP mechanisms in shadow until each has at least
   30 independently resolved episodes. Do not pool candidate rows or retune
   thresholds while evidence accrues.
3. Improve the frozen statistical context layer's ability to distinguish
   direction, whipsaw, and first-touch versus repeat-touch conditions. Evaluate
   it prospectively; do not use historical LLM hindsight.
4. If cost/stop geometry is tested, register one new predeclared prospective
   experiment. Do not reinterpret the spread add-back proxy as proven edge.
5. Do not start USD/CHF or another broad pair campaign merely to increase trade
   frequency. A future pair study needs a new economic mechanism and a locked
   specification before outcomes are inspected.
