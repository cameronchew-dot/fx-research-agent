# Cross-System Edge Forensics

`cross-system-edge-forensics` is a read-only diagnostic joining three evidence
classes without pooling them:

1. immutable non-EUR historical research decisions;
2. the three exact EUR/USD historical lifecycle profiles and their exact-match
   forward status;
3. forward EUR-agent counterfactual outcomes, deduplicated to the first
   deterministic baseline observation in each independent level episode.

The report classifies failures into baseline expectancy, controls, OOS decay,
cost stress, temporal stability, and sample adequacy. It also reports MFE/MAE
and a clearly labeled entry-spread-in-R add-back proxy. That proxy is useful for
diagnosing whether costs may be material; it is not a reconstructed zero-cost
backtest and cannot authorize a geometry change.

Expanded strategy-review mechanisms are shown separately. Candidate-level
figures are descriptive, while the strict linked-episode view is the only
deduplicated forward comparison. Samples below 30 linked episodes are labeled
small and cannot become policy.

The command cannot alter campaigns, lifecycle state, paper state, execution
permissions, or orders.

```bash
python3 ops/research_supervisor.py cross-system-edge-forensics
```
