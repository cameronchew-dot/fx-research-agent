# USD/JPY Intervention Episode Campaign

Campaign 10 is a retrospective mechanism audit, not a tradable signal test.
Japan's Ministry of Finance publishes exact daily intervention dates after the
events. Using those dates as though they were known before entry would create a
look-ahead error.

The locked audit groups same-direction USD/JPY operations into independent
episodes. A new episode begins after at least five consecutive BIS trading days
without intervention or upon a direction change. Policy-aligned movement is
measured from the BIS daily-average close proxy immediately before the first
operation through five trading days after the final operation. Primary and
stress hypothetical costs are 5 and 10 bps.

Controls reverse intervention direction and shift each episode 60 and 120 BIS
trading days forward, excluding shifted windows that contain intervention. The
60/20/20 chronological partitions and all rules are fixed before returns are
inspected.

Even a survivor cannot become a lifecycle profile or order source. It may only
justify a separate, predeclared campaign based on information observable in
real time, such as price stretch, volatility, or public intervention reporting.
